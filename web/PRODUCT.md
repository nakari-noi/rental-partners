# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are flat/apartment owners in Bratislava considering or already doing short-term rental, in one of three situations: an empty or inherited flat with no plan for it; a flat currently rented long-term where the owner wonders if short-term would earn more; or a flat already self-managed on Airbnb/Booking by an owner tired of doing it themselves. They land on the site, compare the two cooperation models, and submit the contact form for a free, non-binding yield estimate.

Guests who book the managed flats are not a user group for this site — they book directly on Airbnb/Booking, not here.

## Product Purpose

Rental Partners manages short-term rental apartments in Bratislava for owners under two models: (A) full management for 10% of turnover — the owner keeps the flat, the company runs it on Airbnb/Booking; (B) guaranteed rent — the company leases the flat from the owner for a fixed monthly rent and sublets it short-term at its own risk. The site's job is to convert a flat owner into a contact-form submission (a free, non-binding yield estimate and site visit).

## Positioning

Personal, founder-led service, not an anonymous agency: the founder (Tomáš) started by self-managing his own Airbnb units for two years before taking on other owners' flats, and owners deal directly with Tomáš or Amanda rather than a call centre. Distinctly, Rental Partners offers owners an explicit choice between two compensation models (percentage-of-turnover management vs. a guaranteed fixed rent) instead of pushing one fixed arrangement — a competing local property manager typically offers only one model and cannot truthfully claim founder-led, hands-on management proven on their own real listings (real, verifiable Airbnb/Booking ratings tied to the founder's own host account).

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive via search or word of mouth, browse Domov → Služby/Cenník/Výsledky/O nás, then submit the Kontakt form with details about their flat (location, size, current status, which model interests them). The company follows up by phone, visits the flat, gives a yield estimate, and proposes a contract. Social proof is central: real Airbnb (Superhost, 4.89/5, 109 reviews) and Booking (9.6/10, 695 reviews) ratings for the founder's existing rental portfolio are shown, with direct links to six live Airbnb listings as portfolio evidence. No booking, payment, or guest-facing functionality happens on this site — it is an owner-acquisition site only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static site (Astro + Tailwind, no CMS, no database). The contact form submits via Web3Forms (third-party email relay to rentalpartners2026@gmail.com); a live API key was still pending at last check. Content is Slovak-only at present; an English version is planned but not yet built (the "SK / EN" control in navigation is currently inert). The operating company, Rental Partners s. r. o. (IČO 57632499, DIČ 2122860663, registered Bratislava – Staré Mesto), was incorporated in May 2026, though the founder's personal Airbnb hosting track record predates the company by about two years — copy is written to credit the team's experience, not the young company's age. Team shown on-site: Tomáš (founder) and Amanda (guest communication); a possible third member is not yet confirmed. Several cooperation specifics (exact payout timing, who covers utilities/cleaning fees, notice periods, whether/how an owner can block dates for personal use) are still being finalized by the client with their advisors and are marked as visible placeholders on the live site rather than invented.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Rental Partners" with a wordmark logo (an overlapping R+P monogram) supplied by the client; the site recolors it into the established palette (ink #2b2118, terracotta #c9694a, cream #f6efe4, sage #6e8b6a) rather than the client's original orange/black artwork. Typography: Outfit (display) + Nunito Sans (body). Voice is direct and numbers-forward, explicitly framed as "partners, not brokers" ("Sme partneri, nie sprostredkovatelia") — never realitná kancelária (real-estate agency) framing.

## Product Principles

1. Numbers over adjectives — real ratings, real review quotes, and real listing links stand in for generic marketing claims everywhere on the site.
2. Two honest paths, not one hard sell — present both compensation models side by side and let the owner self-select instead of steering toward a single option.
3. Never state a fact the client hasn't confirmed — every undecided detail (payout timing, fees, contract terms) stays a visible `[DOPLNIŤ]`-style placeholder, not an invented number.
4. Partner framing, not agency framing — copy consistently distances the company from anonymous "realitná kancelária" positioning in favor of a named, reachable founder and team.
